ABOUT AADHYA

Your features lead. The artistry follows.

Every face has its own balance, structure, and presence. My approach to makeup is shaped by listening to what makes you feel most like yourself — reading the interplay of your bone structure, skin tone, chosen attire, and the light of the venue.

Whether it is the ritual richness of a bridal morning, the romance of an engagement, or an editorial brief, the craft lies in considered skin prep, refined colour harmony, and a weightless finish that stays radiant and effortless throughout your celebration.
